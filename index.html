Hi! I’m Yuchen Su, a senior undergraduate student in mechanics at Tsinghua University.

I am currently advised by Professor Doug James at Stanford Univeristy and Professor Tao Du at Tsinghua Univeristy. Previously, I was also advised by Professor Hongyang Jia at Tsinghua Univeristy.

My research interests lie in physics-based simulation. I have a strong interest in the intersection of mechanics and computer science.
